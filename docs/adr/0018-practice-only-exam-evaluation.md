# ADR 0018 — Practice-only, evidence-grounded exam discovery

- Status: proposed future decision; document consistency reviewed in M12, specialist approval open
- Date: 2026-10-06
- Scope: SPEC F15 / M12 discovery only; no implementation authorization

## Context

Existing conversation feedback and selected A1–B2 do not establish exam scoring
validity. Proprietary tasks, rubrics and recorded media can carry separate rights;
transcripts cannot establish pronunciation or acoustic fluency. M12 must define
a useful future scope before any task, evaluator or interface is implemented.

## Proposed decision

Start a future plan, if accepted, with original general-English constructed
responses and an independently authored, versioned four-dimension practice rubric.
Show qualitative, evidence-grounded feedback and explicit assistance/modality
limitations; no official band/score conversion, CEFR certification or pass claim.
Candidate branded families are discovery options only. Verify current formats,
content/rubric/model-processing rights and qualified review before selecting one.

Validate evidence, revisions and account/source eligibility outside the model.
Abstain when evidence is insufficient. Keep acoustic assessment and raw-audio
storage out of this proposal. Design synthetic fixtures before implementation,
with independent reviewer anchors and policy/isolation cases; fake CI is not
assessment calibration. No automatic update to selected level or adaptive state.

## Alternatives and consequences

Converting current tutoring reports to official bands would offer an attractive
number without rights or calibration evidence; reject that shortcut. Full branded
mock exams would expand modality, format and licensing risks before a small
practice slice is reviewed; defer them. Original tasks reduce rights dependencies
but still require provenance, quality and bias review. Ordinal internal anchors
support fixtures, not a validated measurement scale or public score.

## Acceptance and review triggers

This ADR remains proposed until E1–E4 are resolved for the selected slice in a
separate plan. Revisit for branded material, official-scale estimates, new task
modalities, provider/rubric drift, audio assessment or integration with plans/sharing.
See [exam specification](../m12-exam-practice-spec.md),
[test matrices](../m12-test-matrices.md) and [M12 gates](../m12-discovery.md).
